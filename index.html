dec. 3rd, 2025

this week i went to a concert of one of my favorite artist. their name is yeule. yeule is an experiemntal, electronic/rock artist that bends that space between realtiy and fantasy. i've been a fan of their music for a while now and i ended up meeting them like two years ago so seeing them again was really exciting.

the album evangelic girl is a gun by yeule. a take on experimental rock and electronic music that i've grown to be very fond of recently. this album has honestly been running my life at the moment.

i find yeules music to be one of the most intresting musical items that i've ever encountered. the electronic sounds contrasted to the mostly tragic lyrics that follow is something i find to be very intriguing. in my favorite song by them, "poison arrow", there is only a few lines of lyrics that are repeated throughout the whole song.

"When we were alone, I took your heart. So beautiful. When I fell back. When we were alone, I took your heart. So beautiful. When I fell back."

this exploration of love. the suffocating nature of loving someone so much. stealing their heart. the way they play around with themes of love is my favorite part of their lyrics. i'll get more into this in later entries and explore my idea around love.
